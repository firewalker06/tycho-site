## Capture method and privacy

Product captures use Chrome 155.0.8059.80 at 1440×900, dark scheme, and device scale 1. The capture runner reuses the released `bin/capture-site-quickstart` fixture definitions. It adds only synthetic Welcome Sandbox config, conversation events, a second run, and a typed child report through released domain interfaces. The product renderer is unchanged. All state, config, logs, response style, schedules, and credentials use a separate fixture root. Harness executable overrides point to missing synthetic paths. The server binds only to loopback. No personal sessions, config, URLs, host credentials, or tokens appear in captures. Temporary synthetic paths can be visible in the Welcome Sandbox form.

The stock capture helper first failed on local Ruby/gem and Playwright lookup. The final capture used explicit Ruby/gem paths and the site's installed Playwright with real Chrome. Initial loading-frame captures were rejected and replaced after waiting for actual conversation and Summary content. No product source change was needed.
